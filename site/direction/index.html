Vyre / Direction

Where Vyre is, and where it is going.

A workplace for your team and its AI agents.

AI tools are brilliant in a chat window and lost everywhere else: they don't know your clients, can't pick up a colleague's task, can't safely use your company's accounts, and nothing they do is checked before it goes out. Vyre is the place where they can, on a server you own.

Where Vyre is today · 0.5.0

Released · 0.5

What you can use now

  • People and agents in the same chats@ a teammate or an agent, # a record, a file or a project. Anyone in the Space can make an agent and share it as use-only or full.
  • Agents that wake on a taskAn agent finishes the task, hands it on, or marks it stuck with a plain reason.
  • FlowsTriggers from a person, a schedule, a record change, a stage, a form or a link, and steps that find, create, wait, ask, assign and run an agent. A person approves a Flow before it runs.
  • PlaybooksStages, the tasks in each stage, who does each task, and when a stage counts as done.
  • RecordsContacts, projects, tasks and your own record types, with tags, saved views, and Excel or CSV import.
  • Documents and signingFill your Word templates from a record, as Word or PDF, and send for signature. A signer needs no account.
  • FilesOne Files place for the Space, with linked Google Drive and Dropbox folders.
  • VaultTen groups, shared as Can use or Can edit, and a vault of their own for every member.
  • SitesAn agent builds a site and puts it live with one yes from you.
  • Needs youOne place for approvals, asks and stuck tasks, with typed cards.
  • TeamsRoles, per-project seat levels, invites with a role, and a Space-wide Google or Microsoft 365 key for each member's own mail, calendar and drive.
  • Sign in by nameSign in by name replaces the pairing code, and updates need the owner's yes.

Where Vyre is going

Next · 0.5.1

Flows you can trust

  • One chat per FlowA divider for each run.
  • Deadlines on agent stepsAn agent step that runs too long is stopped and takes a timeout path.
  • A failure panelOn every step in the Flows editor.
  • Per-record privacyFor custom record types.
  • Flow emailWith CC, BCC and attachments.
  • An audit screenFor admins: who changed what on a record.

After that · 0.5.2

Integrations and skills

  • IntegrationsAn Integration wraps an outside app's API and ships its own Flow steps, triggers, connection and agent tools. Clio, Lawmatics, GoHighLevel and DecisionVault are planned.
  • Make an Integration from an APIAn agent can build one for your business on your own server.
  • Skills and PluginsBrowse, write, version and roll back skills. Install and enable plugins per agent and project.

Later

And beyond

  • Screen ShareWatch and take over your agents' computers and browsers from any device.
  • iPhone App StoreThe iPhone app from the App Store, with notifications.
  • A hosted optionA Vyre-hosted home for people without a server.
What does not change

The rules stay where they are.

  • Your machinesVyre runs on hardware you control. Your data lives on your devices and on servers you or your team run. Vyre doesn't hold it. A Cloud space is a server you or your team chose, not ours.
  • Your accountsYour own AI subscriptions and keys. Several per provider.
  • Asking is approvingYour own words are the yes, and some rules no setting removes.
  • Your own work onlyA person’s computer never runs someone else’s work.
  • Nothing feels walled offThe separation is in how it is built, not in how it feels to use.

A plain note

This is direction, not a promise of dates. Order and scope can change when we learn something. The releases are what is true today.

Start with what works today.
