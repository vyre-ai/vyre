The road from 0.2.9

0.2.9 · Now

The first release of the 0.3 work

0.2.9 is the first release of the 0.3 work: Spaces, Wink, the objects layer and the one-yes approvals.

  • SpacesPersonal on your devices, My Cloud on your own server, and Cloud spaces for teams.
  • RecordsContacts, projects, tasks and anything you define, with flows and watchers to run them.
  • ChatsOne place for you, your team and every AI model, with each chat encrypted to the people in it.
  • A built-in networkPair a device with one typed code, with no VPN to install.
  • Lend a computerLend a computer to a team; your work on it is encrypted at rest on your computer and on the server, and deleted from your computer when access ends.

What does not change

The rules stay where they are.

  • Your machinesVyre runs on hardware you control. Your data lives on your devices and on servers you or your team run. Vyre doesn't hold it. A Cloud space is a server you or your team chose, not ours.
  • Your accountsYour own AI subscriptions and keys. Several per provider.
  • Asking is approvingYour own words are the yes, and some rules no setting removes.
  • Your own work onlyA person’s computer never runs someone else’s work.
  • Nothing feels walled offThe separation is in how it is built, not in how it feels to use.

A plain note

This is direction, not a promise of dates. Order and scope can change when we learn something. The releases and the known gaps are what is true today.

Start with what works today.
