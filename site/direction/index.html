Vyre / Direction

Where Vyre is, and where it is going.

A workplace for your team and its AI agents.

AI tools are brilliant in a chat window and lost everywhere else: they don't know your clients, can't pick up a colleague's task, can't safely use your company's accounts, and nothing they do is checked before it goes out. Vyre is the place where they can, on a server you own.

Where Vyre is today · 0.3.0

Released

What you can use now

  • Set up in minutesReserve your name, install the app on your Mac or Windows PC, and add a server: a Linux server or a Mac mini that stays on. Add your Android phone from the app.
  • SpacesPersonal on your devices, My Cloud on your own server, and Cloud spaces for teams.
  • RecordsContacts, projects, tasks and anything you define, with automations and watchers to run them.
  • ChatsOne place for you, your team and every AI model, on your own subscriptions.
  • A built-in networkYour devices reach your server with no VPN to install.
  • One yes before anything goes outWhen an assistant wants to send something, it waits for your approval on your phone.
  • Watch the workSee what an assistant is doing as it happens: its latest thought, each step, and a note when it hands a job to a teammate. The teammate's steps and report come back into the same conversation.
  • One yes for a groupWhen an assistant holds several things, such as three emails, they arrive as one card. You read each item's exact words, drop or edit any, and say yes once. A sent email is logged on the matching client.
  • Connect any app that has an APIUse a key already in your vault, or import the app's API description and approve the connection Vyre drafts. Reading is free; sending, changing and deleting wait for your yes. It works in Flows, watchers and your assistants' tools.
  • Signatures inside VyreInstall Documents to send documents for signature on your own server. A signed document is filed on the client and saved to Drive.
  • Modules that feel built inA module's screens, records, steps and triggers show up in Vyre's own look, and you arrange your own sidebar. Modules you add cannot pair devices or reach another machine.
  • Back online by itselfA Mac server comes back after a power cut, and the app tells you plainly when it cannot reach your server.
  • Backups, with one gapvyre backup carries your vault, settings and files, not the Space's records yet. Keep the server's disk backed up too. That comes in 0.3.1.

Where Vyre is going

Next · 0.3.1

Projects with AI teammates

  • ProjectsA simple project with chats and files, or one made from a template with stages, tasks and AI teammates that do the work and report back.
  • An Engineer in every workspaceSet up templates, automations, screens and connections by talking to it. Every change waits for your approval and can be undone.
  • Skills and pluginsInstall a skill once and every AI and agent in your workspace can use it.
  • Live previewsWhen an agent builds something, a live preview appears in the conversation; share it in one click.
  • Automations you can trustEvery step can prove it worked; failures land in one place with Retry, Skip and Stop.
  • One timelineEvery conversation, email, document and automation that touched a client, in one place.
  • Backups that carry your recordsMoving a Space's records safely needs a re-key step you approve on a device.
  • Your own app modulesAdd your own app as a module, with one approval on your device.
  • Agents and sessions in one viewThe chat view in the app matching Lumen everywhere.

After that · 0.3.2

Documents, messages and publishing

  • DocumentsGenerate documents from your own templates, filled from your records. Sending for signature already works.
  • MessagesEmail and text messages from your own accounts, logged on the client.
  • PublishPut a website or app online on your own domain, with every version kept and one-tap rollback.

  • iPhone App StoreThe iPhone app from the App Store, with notifications.
  • A hosted optionA Vyre-hosted home for people without a server.
What does not change

The rules stay where they are.

  • Your machinesVyre runs on hardware you control. Your data lives on your devices and on servers you or your team run. Vyre doesn't hold it. A Cloud space is a server you or your team chose, not ours.
  • Your accountsYour own AI subscriptions and keys. Several per provider.
  • Asking is approvingYour own words are the yes, and some rules no setting removes.
  • Your own work onlyA person’s computer never runs someone else’s work.
  • Nothing feels walled offThe separation is in how it is built, not in how it feels to use.

A plain note

This is direction, not a promise of dates. Order and scope can change when we learn something. The releases and the known gaps are what is true today.

Start with what works today.
