Work management.

Vyre and Notion.

Notion: a workspace for notes, documents, databases and tasks. Vyre works with it today and does some of its parts natively.

Who it is for, and what it does well

Notion

Teams that want pages, wikis and databases in one flexible workspace.

  • Flexible pages and databases. Notes, wikis and tables share one editor.
  • Easy to shape. People build their own templates and views.
  • Familiar to many people. New teammates often know it already.

Vyre

The agentic command center for AI-native teams: people and agents work in one Space on a server you own.

Industry-neutral. You shape the records, and Flows, Playbooks and a Gate run the work.

Works with Vyre.

How Vyre connects to Notion today.

Today

Notion publishes an API for pages and databases. A person adds a Connection in the Vault that declares the host, how the key is sent and which operations read, draft, change, send, spend or delete. A Flow then calls the service from a web step, or a custom module wraps it.

Where the vendor publishes an MCP server, a person can add it for agents to use.

Next (0.5.2)

A packaged Integration, so the Connection and its operations arrive ready-made.

Vyre can be extended by integrating your Zoho, QuickBooks, RingCentral and the rest of your software stack*, Notion included.

*Where the vendor provides an API.

Where Vyre replaces it.

What Vyre does natively.

Vyre does these parts of the job natively, so a team can drop a seat or a step. Notion stays the better fit for what it was built for.

01

Records

Records of any type you define, linked in any web, with boards, calendars and dashboards.

02

Tasks and Playbooks

Tasks and Playbooks hold the stages of each kind of job and who does each.

03

Agents

An agent takes a task and works in the project, instead of only answering in a page.

Keep Notion for the wiki and long-form writing if your team likes it there. Let Vyre hold the records, tasks and agent work.

Why that helps.

What a team gets from it.

A page records what happened. In Vyre the agent does the next step as well.

  • Agents do the workAn agent takes the task and finishes it, hands it on or marks it stuck with a reason. A person is not moving data between tools by hand.
  • One place for people and agentsPeople and agents are members of the same Space. They share the records, tasks, files and chat, so nobody carries data from one tool to the next.
  • Fewer subscriptions and less copy-pasteOne Space covers parts of a job that took several tools, so there are fewer logins and fewer places to keep in step.
