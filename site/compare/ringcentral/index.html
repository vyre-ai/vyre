Vyre / Compare / RingCentral

Communications.

Vyre and RingCentral.

RingCentral: phone, messaging and video for business. Vyre works with it and does not replace it.

Who it is for, and what it does well

RingCentral

Businesses that run their phone lines, messages and meetings on one communications platform.

  • A business phone system. Numbers, extensions and call handling.
  • Messaging and video together. One platform for calls, chat and meetings.
  • Carrier-grade service. Run by the vendor.

Vyre

The agentic command center for AI-native teams: people and agents work in one Space on a server you own.

Industry-neutral. You shape the records, and Flows, Playbooks and a Gate run the work.

Works with Vyre.

How Vyre connects to RingCentral today.

Today

RingCentral publishes a developer API. A person adds a Connection in the Vault that declares the host, how the key is sent and which operations read, draft, change, send, spend or delete. A Flow then calls the service from a web step, or a custom module wraps it.

Where the vendor publishes an MCP server, a person can add it for agents to use.

Next (0.5.2)

A packaged Integration, so the Connection and its operations arrive ready-made.

Vyre can be extended by integrating your Zoho, QuickBooks, RingCentral and the rest of your software stack*, RingCentral included.

*Where the vendor provides an API.

Where Vyre fits.

What Vyre does around it.

Vyre does not replace RingCentral. These are the parts of the work Vyre does around it.

01

Records

A Flow can turn what comes from RingCentral into a record, where its API allows.

02

Agents

An agent can prepare the follow-up from it.

03

The Gate

The reply waits for a person’s yes.

Vyre does not replace a phone system, and it does not carry calls. Keep RingCentral for telephony and connect it where its API allows.

Why that helps.

What a team gets from it.

The phone system carries the call. Vyre turns it into a record and a task.

  • One place for people and agentsPeople and agents are members of the same Space. They share the records, tasks, files and chat, so nobody carries data from one tool to the next.
  • Agents do the workAn agent takes the task and finishes it, hands it on or marks it stuck with a reason. A person is not moving data between tools by hand.
  • Every outward action approvedAn email, a payment, a deletion or a published page waits at the Gate until a person has read the exact words and said yes.
