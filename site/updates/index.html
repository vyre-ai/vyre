Vyre / Weekly update

The weekly update.

One email a week. What changed in Vyre.

Vyre ships in the open. Each week the project writes down what changed: new releases, what each one does and what comes next. You can get that as one email instead of watching the repository.

Get the weekly update

What the list keeps is on the privacy page.

What you get

  • Nothing is sent yet. When sending starts, the first email asks you to confirm, and only confirmed addresses get the weekly email.
  • One email a week at most, with all of that week's updates in it. Never a second one.
  • The same changes that appear in the releases and the changelog, gathered in one place.
  • A link in every email that removes you in one click. Until then, write to privacy@vyre.run to be removed.

What the list keeps

  • Your name, your email, the time you signed up, the page you signed up on and which version of the promise above you saw.
  • It is used for this email and nothing else. It is not shared or sold.
  • Leaving deletes the row. The privacy page has the whole account.
