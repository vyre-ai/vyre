An example day

People decide. Agents do the work.

Your agent writes this for your firm. Here is the shape of one day, with sample names.

  • 08:10Needs you holds three things for Maya: a reply to a new enquiry, an invoice and a document for signature. Each shows its exact words.
  • 08:25Maya approves the reply and edits one line. The Gate sends it. The invoice waits for a number she wants to check.
  • 10:00A new client signs the engagement letter on their phone, with no account. The signed copy files on the client record and starts the onboarding Flow.
  • 13:30The drafting agent finishes first drafts for five clients. It cannot send. Dev reviews each and says yes or sends it back with a note.
  • 16:45An agent step cannot finish: a document is missing. It marks the task stuck with a plain reason, and the reason is at the top of Dev’s Needs you.

If you want it built

Your agent can set Vyre up with you, over Vyre’s MCP. You install Vyre from the steps: the app shows one install line and a code, and you run and enter them yourself. Your agent then connects: in Claude Code, vyre mcp install prints the line that adds Vyre’s MCP server, and for another MCP client you name a key in Settings, under Your AI keys.

From there your agent works through Vyre’s tools, and Vyre’s built-in Engineer drafts record types, Flows and Playbooks as cards that one yes from you applies. Anything that leaves your team waits at the Gate for your yes. Credentials, sign-ins and devices stay with you.
