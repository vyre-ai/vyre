For consulting firms.

A consulting firm, run as an AI-native firm.

Northfield Advisory is an invented six-person firm that sells diagnostics, programs and retainers. The work is judgment, so the agents carry the research, drafts and status writing, and the partners keep the calls.

The client journey.

Eight steps, one record that carries them.

Northfield Advisory follows one client through the whole firm. Each step names the Vyre pieces behind it. Where a step sends something out, a person approves the exact words first.

  1. 01Inquiry comes in

    A form, an email to a shared mailbox or a referral entered by hand starts a Flow. It finds or creates the organization and the contact, and creates an Opportunity.

    FlowsRecords
  2. 02Research and scoping

    The research agent reads the company’s public material and drafts a one-page brief. The partner reads it before the scoping call.

    AgentsNeeds you
  3. 03Proposal

    The proposal agent assembles the draft from the firm’s past work and price list. The reviewer checks scope and price against the rules in its AGENTS.md. The partner edits and approves.

    AgentsDocuments
  4. 04Statement of work signed

    Documents fills the statement of work from the Opportunity and sends it for signature. The signed copy files on the engagement.

    DocumentsSigning
  5. 05Engagement from a Playbook

    The signature starts the Playbook for the kind of work: diagnostic, program or retainer. Phases, tasks, owners and due offsets arrive together.

    PlaybooksFlows
  6. 06Weekly status

    Every Friday a schedule asks the status agent to read the week’s tasks and decisions for each engagement and draft the client update. The partner approves each one.

    FlowsGate
  7. 07Change requests

    A new request is a record. The Flow asks the partner, updates scope and fee on a yes, and drafts the change note for the client.

    RecordsNeeds you
  8. 08Final report and renewal

    The last phase fills the report template, sends it for the client’s sign-off and opens the renewal Opportunity ninety days later.

    DocumentsFlows

Playbooks.

Each kind of work is its own track.

A Playbook lists stages, the tasks in each stage and who does each task: a person, a role or an agent. A Flow reads the field that names the track and starts the matching Playbook. The tracks run side by side over the same records.

Flows.

Work that runs the same way every time.

A Flow is written once and approved by a person. A trigger starts it, steps do the work, and it stops wherever a person has to say yes. Each run has its own page with a step timeline. In the chains below, outlined is the trigger, tinted is an agent, dashed waits for a person and dark is held at the Gate.

Agents with roles.

The drafting is done. The decisions are yours.

An agent has a job in one sentence, an AGENTS.md that says how to do it and a list of projects it works in. Anyone in the Space can share it with a person or a role as use-only or full. A reviewer is an agent whose instructions say what to check, with a standing duty on an event. It marks a task stuck with a plain reason when it cannot decide, and that task waits under Needs you.

NEEDS YOU

One place for everything that waits.

Approvals, questions and stuck tasks land in one list, on the Mac, the web app and the phone. An agent can ask and cannot approve. The person sees the exact words, approves or edits, and only then does the email, the signature request or the page go out.

Paperwork, secrets and pages.

Your own documents, your own logins, your own page.

01

Documents and Signing

Statements of work, change notes and final reports are the firm’s own Word templates, filled from the engagement. The client signs without an account.

02

Vault

Client data-room logins and survey tools sit in the Vault. Agents use them for one job and never see them. Each consultant has a private vault.

03

Sites

An agent builds a page for each offer and the inquiry form from the firm’s records and brand. It previews at a private address and goes live on one yes.

Connect your stack.

Vyre can be extended by integrating your Zoho, QuickBooks, RingCentral and the rest of your software stack*

Northfield Advisory keeps its tools. Vyre reaches them through the same checks as everything else: the key lives in the Vault, every call is marked read, draft, change, send, spend or delete, and anything that leaves the Space waits at the Gate.

  • ZohoConnectionA Connection and a Flow step today. An Integration is the Next step.
  • QuickBooksConnectionA Connection and a Flow step today. An Integration is the Next step.
  • XeroConnectionA Connection and a Flow step today. An Integration is the Next step.
  • RingCentralConnectionA Connection and a Flow step today. An Integration is the Next step.
  • Google WorkspaceTodayMail and calendar connect per member. Drive folders link as Files.
  • Microsoft 365TodayMail, calendar and files connect per member.
  • DropboxTodayFolders link as Files, read through each person’s own account.
  • DocuSignConnectionVyre signs documents itself. A Connection reaches DocuSign if you keep using it.

*where the vendor provides an API

Today

A Connection plus a Flow step. A person adds a Connection: a declared host, how the key is sent and a list of operations, each marked by what it does. A Flow’s service step calls an operation, and a send waits at the Gate.

Or a custom module. A module is a folder with a module.json and an entry file. It registers tools, emits events and declares Flow steps under the same checks as the built-in modules. An agent can write one on your own server.

Next, 0.5.2

Integrations, packaged. An Integration wraps an application’s API and ships its own Flow steps, triggers, Connection and agent tools. You install, verify, configure and remove it from one place. An agent can make one from an API.

Clio, Lawmatics, GoHighLevel and DecisionVault are the first planned. This is direction, not a date.
