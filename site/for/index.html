Vyre / For

Vyre for your kind of firm.

Your firm, as an AI-native firm.

Our mission is to help every professional services team become AI-native. Pick the kind of firm closest to yours. Each page follows one client from the first inquiry to closed work and shows the records, Playbooks, Flows and agents behind every step.

Explore the interactive mapMap my business

Five journeys

01 Law firms Inquiry, screening and conflict check, consultation, engagement letter signed, matter opened from a Playbook, deadlines from a trigger date, documents from the firm’s own templates, client updates, billing hand-off. Follow the journey 02 Accounting firms Inquiry and scoping, engagement letter signed, client onboarding, document collection with reminders, a monthly close Playbook, tax season tracks, review and delivery, billing and renewal. Follow the journey 03 Consulting firms Inquiry, scoping call, proposal, statement of work signed, engagement from a Playbook, weekly status, change requests, final report and renewal. Follow the journey 04 Marketing and creative agencies Inquiry, discovery and brief, proposal and statement of work signed, onboarding with access in the Vault, a campaign Playbook per offer, creative review rounds, client approval, monthly report, renewal. Follow the journey 05 Real estate brokerages Lead response, qualification, listing or buyer agreement signed, a transaction from a Playbook, key dates from the contract date, documents, client updates, closing and follow-up. Follow the journey

What every journey shares.

One product, shaped to the firm.

The pages differ in their words. The pieces underneath are the same, and you shape them without writing code.

01

Records you shape

As many types as the firm has, joined by links, with views and sealed fields an agent never reads.

02

A Playbook per track

Each kind of work has its own stages, tasks and doers, and they run side by side over shared records.

03

Flows with a person in the loop

Triggers, waits and branches run the same way each time. A send, a signature request or a page waits for a yes.

04

Agents with roles

Intake, drafting, reviewer and more, in one group chat across Claude, Codex, Grok and OpenRouter.

05

Your documents and signing

Your Word templates, filled from records. Signers need no account, and the signed copy files on the record.

06

Your stack, connected

Vyre can be extended by integrating your Zoho, QuickBooks, RingCentral and the rest of your software stack*

*where the vendor provides an API. Today a Connection plus a Flow step, or a custom module. Next, 0.5.2: packaged Integrations.

Ask your agent to map your business.

Insurance agencies, architecture and engineering firms and others follow the same method. Paste this into Claude, Codex or any agent that can read the web. The full prompt lives on the home page.

Copy this into Claude, Codex or any agent

Read https://vyre.run/llms-full.txt and https://vyre.run/docs/for-agents/MAP-YOUR-BUSINESS.md. I run [describe your firm]. We use [your main software]. Interview me about my people, what we track, the kinds of work we do, our paperwork and our software. Then show my business as a Vyre Space: members and roles, record types, a Playbook for each kind of work, Flows, agents with roles, documents and signing, a client page, the apps to connect, and what waits for my yes. Mark what Vyre does today and what is Next. Design on paper and change nothing until I say so.
