For law firms.

A law firm, run as an AI-native firm.

One client, one matter, from the first inquiry to the billing hand-off. Alder Row Legal is an invented two-practice firm, personal injury and estate planning. Every step below uses a piece of Vyre that exists today, and the people keep every decision that matters.

The client journey.

Eight steps, one record that carries them.

Alder Row Legal follows one client through the whole firm. Each step names the Vyre pieces behind it. Where a step sends something out, a person approves the exact words first.

  1. 01Inquiry comes in

    A web form, a call to a Flow’s path or a new email in a watched mailbox starts the intake Flow. It finds or creates the Contact and creates a Lead.

    FlowsRecords
  2. 02Screening and conflict check

    The intake agent fills in practice area and summary, then searches existing clients and parties for the names in the inquiry. A hit goes to the responsible attorney under Needs you. No hit moves the Lead on.

    AgentsNeeds you
  3. 03Consultation

    A Lead entering “Meeting booked” creates an Appointment and drafts the confirmation. The email waits at the Gate until the attorney approves the words.

    FlowsGate
  4. 04Engagement letter signed

    A Lead entering “Qualified” fills the engagement letter from the client record and sends it for signature. The client signs without an account and the signed copy files on the record.

    DocumentsSigning
  5. 05Matter opens from a Playbook

    When the signature event arrives, a Flow reads the practice area, branches and starts the matching Playbook. The matter appears with its stages, its tasks and who does each one.

    PlaybooksFlows
  6. 06Deadlines from a trigger date

    The matter carries dates such as the accident date or the hearing date. A Flow waits until a date field plus an offset, then assigns the task to the attorney with a paralegal following. The reviewer agent checks each date against the rule the firm wrote down.

    FlowsAgents
  7. 07Documents and client updates

    The drafting agent completes missing fields and asks Documents to fill the firm’s own Word template. A stage change drafts the client update, and the attorney approves it before it leaves.

    DocumentsGate
  8. 08Closing and billing hand-off

    A matter entering “Closed” asks the responsible attorney, then assigns the billing task with the matter’s fields filled in. A Connection call can post the same fields to the billing tool.

    FlowsConnections

Playbooks.

Each kind of work is its own track.

A Playbook lists stages, the tasks in each stage and who does each task: a person, a role or an agent. A Flow reads the field that names the track and starts the matching Playbook. The tracks run side by side over the same records.

Flows.

Work that runs the same way every time.

A Flow is written once and approved by a person. A trigger starts it, steps do the work, and it stops wherever a person has to say yes. Each run has its own page with a step timeline. In the chains below, outlined is the trigger, tinted is an agent, dashed waits for a person and dark is held at the Gate.

Agents with roles.

The drafting is done. The decisions are yours.

An agent has a job in one sentence, an AGENTS.md that says how to do it and a list of projects it works in. Anyone in the Space can share it with a person or a role as use-only or full. A reviewer is an agent whose instructions say what to check, with a standing duty on an event. It marks a task stuck with a plain reason when it cannot decide, and that task waits under Needs you.

NEEDS YOU

One place for everything that waits.

Approvals, questions and stuck tasks land in one list, on the Mac, the web app and the phone. An agent can ask and cannot approve. The person sees the exact words, approves or edits, and only then does the email, the signature request or the page go out.

Paperwork, secrets and pages.

Your own documents, your own logins, your own page.

01

Documents and Signing

Engagement letters, fee agreements and client letters are the firm’s own Word templates, filled from the matter and the records linked to it. The signed copy files on the matter and starts the next Flow.

02

Vault

The e-filing login, the bar portal and the court calendar sit in the Vault. An agent uses a credential without seeing it, and each attorney has a private vault the firm cannot read.

03

Sites

An agent builds the firm’s consultation page and intake form from its records and brand, previews it at a private address and puts it live on one yes. Every version stays in the history.

Connect your stack.

Vyre can be extended by integrating your Zoho, QuickBooks, RingCentral and the rest of your software stack*

Alder Row Legal keeps its tools. Vyre reaches them through the same checks as everything else: the key lives in the Vault, every call is marked read, draft, change, send, spend or delete, and anything that leaves the Space waits at the Gate.

*where the vendor provides an API

Today

A Connection plus a Flow step. A person adds a Connection: a declared host, how the key is sent and a list of operations, each marked by what it does. A Flow’s service step calls an operation, and a send waits at the Gate.

Or a custom module. A module is a folder with a module.json and an entry file. It registers tools, emits events and declares Flow steps under the same checks as the built-in modules. An agent can write one on your own server.

Next, 0.5.2

Integrations, packaged. An Integration wraps an application’s API and ships its own Flow steps, triggers, Connection and agent tools. You install, verify, configure and remove it from one place. An agent can make one from an API.

Clio, Lawmatics, GoHighLevel and DecisionVault are the first planned. This is direction, not a date.
