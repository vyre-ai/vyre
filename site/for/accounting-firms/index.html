Vyre / For / Accounting firms

For accounting firms.

An accounting firm, run as an AI-native firm.

A new client, then a year of work on a calendar. Brightwater Accounting is an invented firm with three tracks: monthly bookkeeping, individual returns and business returns. Each track is its own Playbook with its own Flows over the same client records.

Map my businessFollow the journey

The client journey.

Eight steps, one record that carries them.

Brightwater Accounting follows one client through the whole firm. Each step names the Vyre pieces behind it. Where a step sends something out, a person approves the exact words first.

  1. 01Inquiry and scoping

    A form or a call starts a Flow that creates the prospect and a scoping task. The intake agent drafts the questions: entity type, software, volume, year-end.

    FlowsAgents
  2. 02Proposal and engagement letter

    The partner approves the scope. Documents fills the engagement letter from the client record and sends it for signature. The client signs without an account.

    DocumentsSigning
  3. 03Client onboarding

    The signature starts onboarding. A Flow creates the engagement records, assigns the setup tasks and sends the first document request. Tax IDs and bank numbers go in sealed fields.

    RecordsFlows
  4. 04Document collection

    Each request is a record with a due date. A schedule inside business hours finds the overdue ones and drafts a reminder. The reminder waits at the Gate, and the reply starts the next step.

    FlowsGate
  5. 05Monthly close Playbook

    On the first business day a Flow starts the close Playbook for every active monthly client: collect, reconcile, review, report, deliver. Each task has a doer and a checker.

    PlaybooksFlows
  6. 06Tax season tracks

    Individual and business returns each have their own Playbook and stage set. A field on the engagement names the track, and a Flow starts the right Playbook.

    PlaybooksRecords
  7. 07Review and delivery

    The reviewer agent compares the working papers against the firm’s checklist. The partner approves under Needs you, and the delivery email goes out only after a yes.

    AgentsNeeds you
  8. 08Billing and renewal

    Closed work assigns the billing task with fees filled in. A yearly Flow drafts the renewal letter, sends it for signature and moves the engagement to the new year.

    FlowsSigning

Brightwater Accounting is invented. Names, dates and amounts on this page are sample data.

Records.

The things Brightwater Accounting tracks, as records you shape.

Each type has fields in named groups, links to other types, views and sealed values. These are types you define in Customize, or that an agent defines for you over MCP. Fields sit in named groups, and fields marked sealed show an agent a placeholder, never the value.

Playbooks.

Each kind of work is its own track.

A Playbook lists stages, the tasks in each stage and who does each task: a person, a role or an agent. A Flow reads the field that names the track and starts the matching Playbook. The tracks run side by side over the same records.

Flows.

Work that runs the same way every time.

A Flow is written once and approved by a person. A trigger starts it, steps do the work, and it stops wherever a person has to say yes. Each run has its own page with a step timeline. In the chains below, outlined is the trigger, tinted is an agent, dashed waits for a person and dark is held at the Gate.

Agents with roles.

The drafting is done. The decisions are yours.

An agent has a job in one sentence, an AGENTS.md that says how to do it and a list of projects it works in. Anyone in the Space can share it with a person or a role as use-only or full. A reviewer is an agent whose instructions say what to check, with a standing duty on an event. It marks a task stuck with a plain reason when it cannot decide, and that task waits under Needs you.

NEEDS YOU

One place for everything that waits.

Approvals, questions and stuck tasks land in one list, on the Mac, the web app and the phone. An agent can ask and cannot approve. The person sees the exact words, approves or edits, and only then does the email, the signature request or the page go out.

Paperwork, secrets and pages.

Your own documents, your own logins, your own page.

01

Documents and Signing

Engagement letters, management reports and authorization forms are the firm’s own templates, filled from the engagement and the client. Signers need no account, and the signed copy files on the engagement.

02

Vault

Software logins, portal access and shared mailbox passwords sit in the Vault, shared as Can use. The chaser and reviewer agents use a login without seeing it.

03

Sites

An agent builds a page for each service that says what the firm needs each month and how to send it, plus the intake form. It goes live on one yes.

Connect your stack.

Vyre can be extended by integrating your Zoho, QuickBooks, RingCentral and the rest of your software stack*

Brightwater Accounting keeps its tools. Vyre reaches them through the same checks as everything else: the key lives in the Vault, every call is marked read, draft, change, send, spend or delete, and anything that leaves the Space waits at the Gate.

*where the vendor provides an API

Today

A Connection plus a Flow step. A person adds a Connection: a declared host, how the key is sent and a list of operations, each marked by what it does. A Flow’s service step calls an operation, and a send waits at the Gate.

Or a custom module. A module is a folder with a module.json and an entry file. It registers tools, emits events and declares Flow steps under the same checks as the built-in modules. An agent can write one on your own server.

Coming in 0.5.2

Integrations, packaged. An Integration wraps an application’s API and ships its own Flow steps, triggers, Connection and agent tools. You install, verify, configure and remove it from one place. An agent can make one from an API.

Clio, Lawmatics, GoHighLevel and DecisionVault are the first planned. This is direction, not a date.
