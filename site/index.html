Vyre: the agentic command center for AI-native teams

Our mission is to help every professional services team become AI-native.

In an AI-native team, people set the direction and make the calls, and agents do the work: they draft, track, follow up and keep every job moving. Vyre gives both one place to work together, on servers the team owns, where anything that leaves the team waits for a person’s yes.

What you get in 0.5.

One place for the team, and the agents that work with it.

Where the outcome has to be exact, such as who sees a record, what a Flow does or what got sent, declared rules decide and a model does not.

01

Records

Contacts, projects, tasks and the record types you define, with views, tags and Excel or CSV import. A sealed field shows an agent a placeholder, never the value.

02

Flows

Start from a person, a schedule, a record change, a stage, a form or a link. Steps find, create, wait, ask, assign and run an agent. A person approves a Flow before it runs.

03

One group chat

Claude, Codex, Grok and OpenRouter answer in the same chat. Give agents roles: a team that does the work, one model that reviews, one that plans.

04

Playbooks and tracks

A project template holds stages, the tasks in each and who does each task. Run each kind of work as its own track, with its own Playbook and Flows over shared records.

05

Vault

Passwords, API keys, logins with one-time codes, cards, notes and SSH keys. Share an item as Can use or Can edit. Agents use a credential without seeing it.

06

Sites

An agent builds a site, previews it at a private address and puts it live on your yes. Your own domain shows the DNS record to add.

07

Documents

Fill your own Word templates from a record and the records linked to it, as Word or PDF. A model never rewrites the document.

08

Signing

Send a document for signature from a record. The signer needs no account, and the signed copy files back on the record.

One chat · Any model

One group chat. Every AI account you have.

A chat belongs to Vyre, not to one model. Change the model whenever you like and the thread, its files and its recall stay.

  • The model pickerChoose the provider, account, model and effort from the composer.
  • @codex, @grokAdd a name to one message to ask another model. The chat stays where it was.
  • Agents in rolesAnyone in the Space can make an agent and share it with a person or a role as use-only or full. Give one the work, another the review, another the plan.
  • See who answeredEach reply carries its provider’s mark, and a line says “Switched to Grok” when the model changes.

Flows and agents

Work that runs the same way every time.

A Flow starts from a trigger and runs steps. A step can give work to a person or to an agent. The agent wakes with the task, the record and the Flow it came from.

  • Finish, hand on or say stuckAn agent ends a task as done, hands it on, or marks it stuck with a plain reason. A task is never left open in silence.
  • People in the loopA step can name an assignee, an approver and followers. A person approves a Flow before it runs, and every run has its own page.
  • Readable datesText in a step fills in values, such as {today} or {after.starts_at - 1 day as date}, with a date picker to insert them.

04 · Asking is approving

Your words are the approval.

When you ask for something, that is the yes. Touch ID or Face ID is only for pairing, vault secrets and anything that sends, posts or pays on its own.

  • No pop-up for what you askedAn action that matches your request goes ahead. An action nobody asked for waits.
  • A floor no setting removesSome rules cannot be switched off, and an agent cannot loosen one without telling you, with an Undo.
  • Signed releasesUpdates are checked against a pinned key. A box refuses an unsigned or older release and keeps running.

Get started

One line on your server, then pair it from the app.

Reserve your name, install the app, and add a server: the app gives you one line to paste on it and checks four words with you.

Read the steps
  1. Open the Vyre app. Choose your name and create a space.
  2. Paste the line on your server. It asks before it installs anything, including Docker.
    $curl -fsSL vyre.run/i | sh
  3. Check four words in the app. The server prints four words and the app shows four; you confirm they match.
  4. Add your computers. The Vyre app for Mac and Windows pairs to your server.

Any questions?

Straight answers, yes or no first.

Is Vyre free?

Yes. Vyre is open source under Apache 2.0. You use your own Claude, Codex, Grok or OpenRouter account, so you pay those providers directly and nobody pays Vyre.

Do I need a server?

Yes, one machine that stays on: a Linux server with Docker, or a Mac that stays on. Your Mac, your Windows PC and your phone connect to it. Your agents keep working when your laptop is closed.

Does it run on Windows?

Yes, as an app on your PC with a tray icon and an Alt+Space panel. The server itself runs on Linux or on a Mac that stays on. The Windows app is not code-signed yet, so Windows asks you to choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on Android and iPhone. You install the Vyre app from a file (it is not on a store yet), scan the code your computer shows, and check that both screens show the same words.

Where does my data live?

Records, chats, files and the vault stay on your server and your computers. vyre.run holds your name’s DNS record and runs the relay, which carries phone pairing, end-to-end encrypted. Prompts go to your AI provider the way they would from that provider’s own app.

Do I need a VPN or another network app?

No. Vyre has its own private network built in, so your server, your computers and your phone find each other with nothing to install and nothing to sign in to. Where a direct path is not possible, Vyre’s relay carries the connection, end-to-end encrypted.

Does it replace Claude Code, Codex or Grok?

No. Vyre runs them with your own accounts and adds records, Flows, a vault, agents with roles and one chat that outlives any one model. You keep using each tool the way you do.

What stops an agent from doing something I did not ask for?

Anything that leaves your server, such as an email, a payment, a deletion or a published page, waits at the Gate until a person has read the exact words and approved them. An agent can ask and cannot approve. A model sees a placeholder where a secret would be. The architecture page shows how.

Who is Vyre for?

Professional services teams that want people and agents to work in one place: the people set the direction and make the calls, and the agents draft, track and follow up. Point your AI agent at the repo and it can show how your business would run on Vyre.

Is it ready?

It is 0.5.0, and what this page shows works today. What comes next is labelled with its version on the direction page and in the roadmap.
