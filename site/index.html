07The floor

Rules no setting can switch off.

Vyre enforces these outside the model, in hooks and in the gate every request goes through. The model can't talk its way past them.

  1. Nothing goes out as you until you've seen the final words.
  2. You see where something is going before it goes.
  3. Every file change shows, including ones a command made quietly.
  4. Vyre can show the source of anything it tells you.
  5. No vault value appears on any screen, log or event.
  6. The Capsule works offline on your own Mac.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Vyre is open source under Apache 2.0. You bring your own Claude subscription or an Anthropic API key, and your agents run on it.

Where does my data live?

On your box and your Mac. vyre.run only holds one DNS record for your name, so your address can find your box. None of your sessions, memory or vault pass through it.

Do I need a server?

A Linux box with Docker is the best home for Vyre, since your agents keep working when your Mac sleeps. You can also start on your Mac and add a box later.

Does it replace Claude Code?

No. Vyre plugs into Claude Code as a plugin, with one small daemon beside it. You keep using Claude Code, with memory, a vault, agents and the Capsule around it.

What is Tailscale for?

Tailscale joins your box, your Mac and your phone into one private network. Your address only opens from devices on it, and Tailscale already knows it's you, so there is no separate login. The free plan is enough.

Is it ready?

It's 0.1.0, which means early. What this page shows works today, except what carries a coming chip. Expect rough edges, and tell us about them on GitHub.

Does it run on Windows?

Not yet. The box runs on Linux and the Capsule on macOS. The Deck opens in any browser on your network.
