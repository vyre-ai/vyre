Vyre / Mac

Vyre on Mac

The Vyre app, in its own window on your Mac.

Download it, open it, and the app walks you through connecting to your server. Your agents run on your server; the Mac app is the window.

Set up VyreRead the steps
What you get

The same app, with a Mac window.

  • One windowOpens on launch and when you open the app again. The menu bar icon has Open Vyre and Quit Vyre.
  • Touch IDApprove what needs your yes with Touch ID.
  • Your filesDrop a file onto the app to hand it to an agent.
  • Your serverEverything runs on your own server; the app connects to it.
Install

Download the app, then connect it.

Get the Mac app from the latest release.

  1. Download it. Vyre-aarch64.dmg for Apple silicon or Vyre-x86_64.dmg for an Intel Mac, from the latest release. Open it and drag Vyre to Applications.
  2. Open it once. If macOS says it cannot check the app, choose Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy and Security.
  3. Follow the app. It asks whether to connect to your server or set one up, and walks you through it.
What it needs
MacmacOS 14 or newer
ServerA Linux server, or another Mac that stays on
AIOne of Claude, Codex, Grok or OpenRouter

A Mac that stays on can be your server too: run the install line in Terminal.

Known gaps in 0.5.0

  • The Mac app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, so you open it once with Open Anyway.
Questions

Questions about Vyre on Mac.

Do I need the Mac app to use Vyre?

No. Your agents run on your server and the Vyre app opens in any browser.

Can my Mac be the server?

Yes, a Mac that stays on can be the server. A Linux server is the best home, because agents keep working when a laptop sleeps.

Does the app need Touch ID?

Only to approve what needs your yes.
