Vyre 0.3.0 makes your assistants easier to watch and easier to say yes to, lets you connect any app that has an API, adds Documents for signatures, and lets modules look and feel like part of Vyre.

## Working with your assistants

- **Watch the work.** Lumen and the app's chat show what an assistant is doing as it happens: its latest thought, each step ticking from running to done, and a note when it hands a job to a teammate ("Asked kit"). The teammate's steps and its report come back into the same conversation, even if you closed it and opened it again.
- **One yes for a group.** When an assistant holds several things for your approval, such as three emails, they arrive as one card. You read each item's exact words, drop or edit any, and say yes once. On iPhone that is one Face ID for the whole group. On Android the phone still asks for your fingerprint for each item. A call that is too long to show in full is read to the end first and approved on its own.
- **A sent email is logged on the client.** When an email goes out, it becomes an activity on the matching client record, with the subject and the first words, not the whole message.
- **A notice when something is done.** If you are not looking, Vyre tells you when something waits for your yes and when a turn has finished. The app makes the notice itself; nothing goes through a push service. An iPhone shows it when the app opens.
- **Chat.** Copy and Copy as Markdown on every answer, Copy code on code blocks, find in a conversation with Cmd or Ctrl and F, a clearer header, and your message shows at once while it is sent. You can ask another model about an answer, and it sees the question, the answer and what came before.

## Connect anything

- **Connect any app that has an API** from a key already in your Vault. Vyre checks it and tells you in plain words whether it works. Reading is free; sending, changing and deleting wait for your yes.
- **Describe an app once.** Import its API description (OpenAPI, Postman, as JSON or YAML, from a file or an address) and Vyre drafts the connection for you to approve. An assistant can propose a connection too, but only you can make it.
- **Use it everywhere.** A connection works in Flows, in watchers that read it on a schedule, and in your assistants' tools.

## Documents

- **Signatures, inside Vyre.** Install Documents from Apps to send documents for signature and collect signed copies. It runs on your own server, at its own address.
- **Filed on the client.** When someone signs, the signed document is filed as a Document record on that person's client record and shows on their timeline, and the signed PDF is saved to Drive.
- **Send from a client.** A client with an email address has a Send for signature button.
- Documents is built on DocuSeal (AGPL-3.0), credited in Settings, About, Open-source credits.

## Modules that feel built in

- **Screens in Vyre's look.** A module describes its screens (lists, details, forms, boards, summaries) and Vyre draws them in the app, so they look and behave like Vyre's own. A module's screens can show and act on a connected app's operations.
- **A sidebar you arrange.** Pin Vyre's places, a module's screens and saved views; group, reorder and hide them. The space owner can set the default.
- **Records.** A module can bring its own record types, ready to use.
- **The # picker.** A module can offer its own things to the # picker in chat. What a tag gives an assistant is words only, never more access.
- **Flows.** A module can add its own steps and triggers to Flows.
- Modules you add cannot pair devices, change who can reach your server, or reach another machine. Only Vyre's own modules do.

## Always back online

- A Mac server comes back by itself after a power cut and does not sleep. The installer checks FileVault first and says plainly what it means: with FileVault on, the Mac waits at its login window after a restart.
- If the app cannot reach your server for a minute, it says so and what to check ("Your server has been offline since 14:02"), instead of a screen that hangs.

## Smaller changes

- **Mac sessions start again for more setups.** A sandboxed assistant session on a Mac now finds node wherever it lives (Homebrew, nvm, Volta, fnm), so a Claude Code installed with npm starts instead of failing its safety check.
- **Vault in plain words.** Approval prompts say what is being done ("Save Juniper Drive"), empty required fields say so under the field, and a saved item no longer asks you to unlock.

- **Update button.** Now shows when a new Vyre is out, and Settings, About has one button: Check for updates, or Update.
- **One Keychain prompt.** The Mac app keeps its keys as a single Keychain item, so it asks once instead of several times.
- A request you approved on your phone now goes through when the assistant tries again. Before, it was refused.
- The installer waits for the four words on a first start, and says it is waiting. `vyre` commands that need Docker tell you to use sudo when you cannot reach Docker.
- The Add a server line ends after the four words.
- The Android app is signed with Vyre's own release key.
- Planner tasks can escalate: a task you set to escalate puts a "Late" to-do in front of the person you named.

## Not yet

These are planned for 0.3.1:

- **Backups and your records.** `vyre backup` and restore carry your Vault, settings and files, but not the Space's records (clients, tasks, Flows' data). Records are sealed to the Space's own keys, and moving them safely needs a re-key step you approve on a device. Until then, keep the server's disk backed up too (issue #108).
- Your own app modules (Part 3), and Windows Home.
- Agents and sessions together in one view, and the chat view in the app matching Lumen's everywhere.
- Notices on Android while the app is closed. They arrive while the app is running in the background.
- Pasting or dropping an image or file into the composer for every model.
- A full timed bench of the chat.

## The apps

The Mac app and the Android app are attached to this release. Both are sideloaded for now:

- **Mac:** open the dmg and drag Vyre Lumen into Applications, then open it. If macOS says it cannot check the app, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and choose Open Anyway.
- **Android:** download Vyre-android.apk on your phone and open it. Allow installs from that source when Android asks.
- **iPhone:** there is no App Store app yet; vyre.run/phone has the steps.

## Updating

A server updates from Settings, Updates, or with `vyre update`. Your data, vault and sign-ins stay as they are.
