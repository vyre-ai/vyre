Vyre 0.2.11 finishes the fix for your server's own address: <name>.vyre.run gets its certificate and opens in a browser.

## Fixed

- Your server now waits until the internet can see its certificate check before asking for the certificate. In 0.2.10 it asked too early, so the certificate was still refused.

## Updating

A Linux server updates from Settings, or with `vyre update`. The stable channel takes this release. Your data, vault and sign-ins stay as they are.
