Vyre 0.6.9 makes chats reliable: replies show live, long answers finish, and a server that is gone no longer freezes the app.

## Fixed
- An open chat shows the agent's reply as it arrives. Before, a reply only appeared after closing and reopening the chat, and your own message could stay on "Sending".
- A reply written while the connection was briefly down arrives when it reconnects.
- A long answer finishes. Vyre ended the agent's session 30 seconds after the AI's first "done", while it was still working, and dropped the rest. It now waits until the AI has been quiet for 30 seconds.
- Try again after "Couldn't resume" resends your last message and gets the answer.
- A paired server that does not answer no longer holds up the app. It shows "Not answering", and its menu has "Remove this Space from this computer". Settings, Devices and computers lists every server, offline ones too.
- The usage line names the limit it means, with that limit's own number, and gives the reset as a date and time in your time zone.
- The app stops repeating requests that fail.
