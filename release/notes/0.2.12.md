Vyre 0.2.12 is a new way to set up Vyre: reserve your name on the web, then do everything else in the Vyre app.

## Setting up

1. Reserve your name at vyre.run/setup. You get a code.
2. Install the Vyre app on your Mac or Windows PC from the downloads below, then paste the code. The app makes your key on that computer, and the key never leaves it.
3. Choose how you use Vyre:
   - **Join a team:** paste the invite your team sent you. You use their server, so you need none of your own.
   - **Add a server:** a Linux server, or a Mac that stays on. Choose With Records (recommended) or Without Records, run the one line the app shows, and confirm the four words in the app.
   - **My Home (Mac):** Vyre runs on your Mac while it is awake. You can add a server later and move everything across.
4. Add your phone from the app.

## What changed

- Pairing a server or a phone now finishes every time. Before, a typed code could leave the app waiting for ever, and one failed try blocked the next.
- A Mac can be your server. On a Mac server, your Mac app's Touch ID key approves new devices.
- On a Windows PC, your key is made with Windows Hello.
- Your phone is added from your computer, and your computer approves it.
- A team space is made in three steps: name it, add its server, invite people (or skip).
- Records spaces no longer refuse calls while their store is starting.
- Your server's own address gets its certificate.
- The installer speaks plainly. It no longer floods the screen with Docker's output, and `vyre words` shows your four words again.
- The vyre.run/setup page now only reserves your name. Setup itself happens in the app.

## The apps

The Mac app and the Android app are attached to this release. Both are sideloaded for now:

- **Mac:** open the dmg and drag Vyre Lumen into Applications, then open it. If macOS says it cannot check the app, open System Settings, Privacy and Security, and choose Open Anyway.
- **Android:** download Vyre-android.apk on your phone and open it. Allow installs from that source when Android asks.
- **iPhone:** there is no App Store app yet; vyre.run/phone has the steps.

## Updating

A server updates from Settings, Updates, or with `vyre update`. Your data, vault and sign-ins stay as they are.
