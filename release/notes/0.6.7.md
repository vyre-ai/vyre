Vyre 0.6.7 makes Vyre one app with five places.

## Changed

- The sidebar is Home, Chats, Projects, Records and Files, with Vault and Settings at the foot. Apps, Sites, Agents, Flows, Calendar, Memory and Build are no longer places; you reach agents and Flows from Chats and Projects.
- Home shows Needs you, Doing, Today (with a Week view), Tasks and Recent. The Planner and the separate Needs you and Doing pages are folded in.
- A project's tabs are Overview, Chats, Tasks, Files, Flows, Team, Memory, Site and Timeline. A project's site is its Site tab.
- Each agent has one page. Old agent and assistant addresses open it.
- Settings has two parts: the Space you are in (its name is the heading), and Everywhere (sign-in, devices and computers, About). Rules and Groups sit on People and roles, Servers on Devices and computers, and Outside, Spend and Permissions on What agents may do.
- The Vault is one page. Folders and Advanced open as sheets on it.
- Your theme and your notification switches are yours in each Space; other people's choices no longer change yours.
- Records: tags show as saved views, and a record can draft a document, send it for signing, or send an email.

## New

- An agent's computer opens full screen from the Computer tab in the pane on the right.
- On a phone, you can save Vyre to your home screen.
- Windows: the app remembers its window, uses the system font and drops the extra scrollbar.
- `vyre doctor` counts the last day's errors, warnings, slow calls, retries and module restarts.

## Fixed

- The actions that show when you hover a message sit just above it and no longer cover a short message.
- A message you send shows on your side at once, as sent; "Sending" appears only when the server is slow.
- Sending in a chat with an agent works when two servers are paired. It was refused with "Vyre does not know which Space this belongs to".
- Chats shows only your chats. The Engineer's chats (a new site, a new flow) and Flow runs no longer flash in the list when it opens.
- Devices loads on a paired computer instead of answering that it cannot tell who you are.
- A server start no longer warns about six modules' old version field.
