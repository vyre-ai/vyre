Vyre 0.3.3-rc.2 is a release candidate on the beta channel, for testing before 0.3.3. Do not put a team on it: rc.3 is the release for teams.

## What to test

- A team in one Space: the owner makes the team in the app and it is the server's own Space. Members join, share Records, and each has their own chats.
- A shared chat with three people: each person's message runs on that person's own AI sign-in, messages sent while a turn is running wait their turn and are all answered, and nobody's turn uses another person's sign-in. A member can sign in with their own Codex or Grok from Settings, AI accounts, and with their own Claude when the server runs on Linux.
- A member's own projects: a member starts a project of their own, blank or from a template the owner installed, up to 50 live ones. They list, read and upload files in the files area of a project they own or are on (150 KB up, 1.4 MB down).
- A member's own skills, and Flows by proposal: a member drafts and approves their own skills, and sends a Flow set up by someone else for approval instead of approving it.
- The vault from a member's side: a member who asks for the owner's vault is told whose it is and what to do instead.
- A person's own session on their own computer, handed to the server and taken back.

## Fixed since 0.3.3-rc.1

What a person sees and changes:

- A member's turn can no longer run on another person's AI sign-in by attaching a picture or by mentioning a provider without an account.
- Messages queued behind a running turn in a shared chat are all answered, in order, each under its own person. A message queued in a team Space chat stays in that chat. One that cannot run says so in the chat.
- A person's skills are loaded for that person's turn, never the chat starter's or the owner's.
- A member can make an agent of their own, and change, stop or remove only an agent they made.
- Shell commands that a Grok or Codex session runs through Vyre now start on a server, and they hold no AI sign-in.
- A total over linked records, such as a fee, is worked out for the owner even when the type holds a private record.
- "Set up My Cloud" on a server whose Space is a team's is refused with a next step: connect another server.
- With about nine sessions at once the server no longer stalls.
- Lumen on a Mac no longer quits when the server sends an ordinary nested event, and refuses one nested past 64 levels.
- The first Records write to a new kind of record no longer waits minutes.
- The product is called Lumen in the words a person reads.
- A team member's notes, reminders, goals, previews, planner rows and templates are no longer open to other members, and a member cannot change agents, templates or playbooks.
- A project's memory needs a seat on the project.
- The project list and a project's story (the project tools) are given to a member only for projects she owns or is on. Her AI can still read every project record through Records until rc.3.
- Flows that could not set up their record types during a slow first install now keep trying until the records answer, and say so in the meantime.
- A member reads a team chat in her app.

Who can see and change what, as rules:

- A person's reminders, notes and goals are read by that person, and by the owner and admins.
- A member's to-dos go only into projects they are on, and a member changes, finishes or removes only to-dos filed for them.
- A task is changed, or a change to it is asked for, only by its doer, whoever gave it, or the owner or team of its project.
- Agents, templates and playbooks are changed only by an owner or an admin. A member reads them.
- A member sees an agent's name, kind, model and status, not its instructions, tools or sign-in.
- A project's memory is read only by people on its team, and by the owner and admins. Naming a project does not open it.
- An AI session cannot read the server's internal event stream.

## Known issues

rc.2 is for one person's own install. Do not put a team on it: rc.3 is the release for teams, and closes the first four items below. Testing a team with test people and test data is what this candidate is for. Real team data is not.

- A team member can read, change and remove the records of chats, file shares and logged communications she is not part of. The messages and files themselves are not open. Do not put private material in a team's Records that members should not see.
- A team member can see the team-member list and every project record in the Space through Records, and open projects she is not on.
- A team member can see every task through the tasks list, including the owner's own.
- A team member sees in her event log that a file was written, and its path, for files she cannot open.
- On a device that joined a team, Now, Tasks, Projects, Records, Calendar and Tags show her own home's things, not the team's (the team's reach her AI and the Flows, chats and project files screens). Fixed in rc.3.
- Contacts, organizations and team calendar events are shared by design: members can change and delete them, and deleted ones are in the Bin.
- A member's app does not list contact points, subscribers, appointments or leads, but a member can still see them through her AI and Records.
- A member's own paired phone or computer reads the server's event feed, which carries titles of other people's items. Pair only devices you trust.
- Agents and agent teammates answer only the owner and admins. A member's request to one is refused with a next step.
- A project a member starts creates no first task for them.
- A member's app is limited to 120 calls a minute, and a screen that needs more shows less.
- `vyre call` typed inside an AI agent's terminal is refused. Agents reach Vyre's tools through the connection Vyre gives them, which works.
- First install with Records: from about seven minutes to about half an hour, depending on the server, from Records being up until every module's record types exist, and the hello during set-up does not stream. Until then the app says it is waiting for the records, and a first chat can take about 20 seconds.
- Lending a person's own computer to a team, and handing a session to the phone, are not built yet. Handing a session between your own computer and the server has been tested on one machine only.
- A project started from the library template gets no Flow.
- A person removed from a chat is cut off from its live activity at the next call anyone makes into that chat, not at the instant of removal.
- A second Space on one server can still be made by resuming a creation begun before this version, and exists on servers that made one earlier. Those keep working.
- On a server, an assistant with no AI sign-in picked for it runs on the server's own sign-in for the owner's requests. A member's request to such an assistant is refused.
- The command is still `vyre capsule`; a `vyre lumen` name comes later.
- Servers on rc.1 are not upgraded by this release. Install it fresh.

## Updating

This release is on the beta channel only. A server on the stable channel stays on 0.3.1.
