Vyre 0.6.8 makes the core work end to end: chat, threads, agent computers, Records on Vyre's own store, Flows, Files and sign-in, with a calmer Home, Records page and profile.

## Chat and threads
- Starting a chat with an agent, and Home's Ask, work with two servers paired. They were refused with "Vyre does not know which Space this belongs to".
- Every chat with an agent is one of its threads. The Threads tab lists them, newest first; New thread starts one. The sidebar keeps one row per agent.
- A thread is titled by what you first typed, and the header shows the agent's name and face.
- When your AI account hits its limit, the turn carries on with your next signed-in account and says which.
- Switching model mid-chat keeps the conversation. In a group, each reply shows the provider that answered.
- Search finds words in any chat or thread you can read, not only the ones open on this computer.
- A message you send sits on your side at once. Hover actions sit just above a message and no longer cover it.
- Chats shows only your chats: no Engineer, no Flow runs flashing in.

## Agent computers
- The Computer tab is just the screen. Press it to wake; press a live screen to open it full screen, with Take over, Sign in privately, close and Esc.
- An agent uses its own Chrome, Terminal and Files when you ask, and you watch it live.
- `vyre update` brings the computer image with it.

## Records
- Records live only in Vyre's own store. Twenty is gone, and updating removes its old containers. Existing test Spaces start empty.
- Switching Spaces shows that Space's records.
- The record page is calm: the ID once, small, with a copy button; Call, Meeting, Note, Task; a plain property list; Add a property; Connections and History as plain sections. Contacts have no avatar.

## Home, profile and sign-in
- Home is a calm column: Ask under the greeting, your agents as chips, and only the sections that have something in them.
- Your profile: upload your own photo, edit your name in place, copy your username. The first run asks for a photo the same way.
- Continue with GitHub fills your name and photo. Settings, Devices lists your computers.

## Flows and Files
- Ask an agent to make a Flow and it appears under the project. Flows run on a schedule, a webhook (with its address to copy) or a watcher; a step can have a deadline; a failed run says why and offers Retry.
- Files: Shared with me, Word, Excel and PowerPoint previews, moving and renaming folders, and a second writer waits instead of overwriting.

## Server
- A fresh Ubuntu or Debian server installs with one command, Docker included. Agents idle for 15 minutes sleep and wake on the next message. `vyre doctor` runs to the end and counts the last day's log.

## Also
- About forty smaller fixes across chat, vault, settings, sign-in, records and files.
