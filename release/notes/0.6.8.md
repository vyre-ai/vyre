Vyre 0.6.8 fixes chatting with an agent when two servers are paired.

## Fixed

- Starting a chat with an agent, and Home's Ask your assistant, were refused with "Vyre does not know which Space this belongs to" when two servers are paired. They now go to the server of the Space you are in.
- `vyre doctor` no longer stops partway with "Cannot access 'lines' before initialization".
