Vyre 0.2.10 makes your server's own address work: <name>.vyre.run now gets its certificate and opens in a browser.

## Fixed

- In 0.2.9 a server never got the certificate for its address, so <name>.vyre.run did not open. It does now.

## Updating

A Linux server updates from Settings, or with `vyre update`. The stable channel takes this release. Your data, vault and sign-ins stay as they are.
