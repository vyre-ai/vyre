Vyre 0.2.9 puts your team, your records, your chats and your agents in one place on your server, with every act that leaves it waiting for a person's yes.

## New

- Spaces for your team, with roles (owner, admin, manager, member and temp) and a Personal Space of your own. One approval moves your Personal Space to your own server (My Cloud).
- Records your people and agents share: contacts, organizations, leads, clients, appointments, projects, tasks, and every email and meeting, linked both ways. Shape them with your own types, fields, stages and views, or install a Kit.
- Sealed fields: a value such as a social security number is held by Vyre's sealing process, and an agent sees only a placeholder.
- Flows: watch for a new email, a form answer, a stage change or a time, then run declared steps. Every outside write is safe to repeat, and anything that sends, pays, publishes, shares or deletes waits for one yes.
- One chat for people, assistants and models: @ a member or an agent, # a record, switch the model a reply comes from. A chat with a person in it is encrypted with a key that lives on its members' devices.
- Exact recall: read a past part of a chat word for word, including your terminal Claude Code sessions.
- Pair a device three ways: scan the code, paste it, or type the short code it shows.
- Agents hand work to each other: ask your assistant, and it gives the task to the agent best placed for it.
- A new mark: the halftone V, on the app icon, the splash and the site.

## Fixed

- A first install no longer waits for the records database before the server answers, and a module that started before the database was ready starts again when it is.
- Asking an agent from your phone or your Mac works with the kernel on, and the keyboard comes back to you after the agent replies.
- A paired drive joins your Space's storage again.
- An approval card names the device that asked.
- The My Cloud upgrade goes ahead when My Cloud runs on another server.
- `vyre uninstall` stops every Space's containers, and keeps their data.

## Not yet

- Switching a chat back to a model it had before resumes that model's own session, which did not hear the turns in between. Fixed in 0.3.0.
- When My Cloud runs on another server, your chats stay in your Personal Space, readable there. Carried across in 0.3.0.
- Nothing has been walked on a real iPhone or Android yet, and native push notifications are not set up. See [Known gaps](../../docs/known-gaps.md).

## Updating

A Linux server updates from Settings, or with `vyre update`. Your data, vault and sign-ins stay as they are.
