Three ways to draw a wire

Shown at 240 / 32 / 16 px, on graphite and on paper
A
Recommended
Lead

One wire bent into a v. The signal has left the end of the line and is on its way.

B
Port

A closed network with one opening. A single wire comes in and ends at your machine.

C
Trace

A flat line with one pulse on it, lit in signal. The v is the event, not a letter.
