Step 4 of 6 · Your address

Your address.

Vyre reserves the name, points it at this machine's tailnet address, and gets a certificate for it. Only your tailnet can open this address.

https://alex.vyre.run
  1. Reserve alex.vyre.run
  2. Point it at this machine on your tailnetalex.vyre.run → 100.101.12.7
  3. Get the certificateLet's Encrypt, renews in 60 days

From here the loopback link stops working. The rest of the setup continues at your address.
