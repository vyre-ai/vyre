Rings on every device. The first one you answer clears the rest. If nobody answers, it rings again every 5 min, 3 times at most.
How it works
1Six kinds: alarm, timer, reminder, todo, note, event. Todos carry a list, a priority from P0 to P3, a due time and subtasks. Notes are markdown, with a pin and tags.
2Quick add parses plain words and shows the result as a chip before you commit. Ambiguous input gets a reason instead of a guess: "Which Friday? This week or next".
3Google calendars are read-only and say so on the event. Invites to other people wait at the Gate and show in Needs you, with the violet dot only.
4"Added by kit" appears only when an agent other than juno added the item. Items fired while the box was down come back marked missed.
5Firing: Done, Snooze, Dismiss. Alarms and timers ring through quiet hours; reminders and todos wait. Each device keeps the next 48 h of alarms, so they ring offline.
6The lock screen shows a fixed title only: Alarm, Reminder, Timer finished, Starting soon, Todo due. The item name stays inside the app.
