Rings on every device. The first one you answer clears the rest. If nobody answers, it rings again every 5 min, 4 rings in all.
How it works
1Six kinds: alarm, timer, reminder, todo, note, event. Todos carry a list, P0 to P3, a due time and subtasks.
2Quick add is rule-based, no model call. The chip shows kind, time, repeat and duration and updates as you type. No match, no chip.
3Google events say "Google · work · read-only". All-day events sit in a strip at the top; a meeting link shows as Join.
4"from kit" shows only when an agent other than juno added it. Agents never add events; invites wait at the Gate.
5Unanswered, a reminder rings again every 5 min, 3 more times: "Ringing again · 2 of 4". Events ring once and never repeat. Rings missed while the box was down come back marked.
6Each device schedules the next 48 h of rings itself, box or not. "Live" means the stream is connected; offline, the pill takes its place. Lock screen: a fixed title (Alarm, Reminder), never your words, unless you turn on labels.
