How it works
1One tip at a time, from tips.next. The surface calls tips.seen when it draws one. None to show means nothing: no empty slot, no reserved height.
2Tip line: a 12 lightbulb, the text, Show me when the tip has docs or a command, and a close ×. The chip holds the same parts at 28, radius 14, max width 560, truncating with an ellipsis.
3Backticked parts render as their kind: a key as a key chip, a command in JetBrains Mono 12 --text-2.
4Hidden at once while busy (an ask, a draft, a running turn, typing) and while waiting.count is above 0. A tip never sits over a question, an approval, a plan or the waiting list, and never moves them.
5Never in the tab order and never takes focus. On the Deck, F6 moves focus into the footer region first. The line is role="note", never announced.
6No tip in Glass or during a take-over. The CLI prints a dim "tip:" line on stderr after a command succeeds.
7Smooth: in with an opacity change over 150 after the view settles, never during a load or a stream. Out over 150. Nothing slides; nothing pulses.
