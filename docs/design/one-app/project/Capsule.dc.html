How it is built
1The Capsule's colours and sizes come from the same tokens.json, exported to Swift as Theme.swift. Instrument Sans is bundled; icons draw at 16 and 20.
2The list under the input is the Needs row from the app, trimmed to one line of meta. A and D answer the focused row without opening anything.
3The menu bar mark turns its dot violet while something waits. Nothing else in the bar changes colour.
4Only sends ask for Touch ID, and one proof covers 30 min. Allowing an ask never does.
