How it works
1Grow to the body: the card grows with its content, line by line, from its thinking height. It never has a fixed height, and there is no maxHeight smaller than the body.
2472 at most: the body between the input and the footer is 472 (560 - 56 - 32), less the collapsed results line and the waiting list when they show. Past that the card scrolls inside.
3What stays put: the input, the card's header and the footer. A 4 wide thumb in rule-strong shows at the card's right edge, 3 in, while it can scroll. The last visible line is always whole.
4Keys, with the field focused and nothing below the card selected: ⌘↑ ⌘↓ go to the top and bottom, PageUp PageDown move a page, ⌥↑ ⌥↓ move three lines. The trackpad and the wheel scroll the card under the pointer.
5Following: while the answer streams the card follows the newest line. Scroll up and it stops; "Jump to latest ⌘↓" shows at the card's bottom edge until you return to the bottom or press it.
6Ease once: the panel's height eases once, over Motion.reveal (150 ms), when the card first needs more room, and never while text streams or a key repeats.
7No empty headings: a group header draws only when its group has at least one row. Never a "Sends to", "Commands" or any other heading with nothing under it.
8The tests: a snapshot of a 60-line answer shows the last line whole, the thumb, and no text cut at the card's bottom edge; a second shows the top after ⌘↑.
