How it works
1Vyre owns the session (ADR 0030). The chip names the provider the router picked, the model and the auth; a limit fallback to the API key shows as a line in the turn. The state word is one of starting, idle, running, waiting, stopped, failed.
2Beautified, not hidden: every tool call is one quiet row with a verb and a mono summary. A diff opens in place, additions on the lime wash, deletions on a neutral wash. Thinking folds to its length and opens to the reasoning in muted text.
3It works like Claude Code in the terminal. Typing while kit works steers: ⏎ hands the words to kit at its next step (SDK priority "next") and a quiet "you steered here" row marks where they landed. ⌥⏎, or a long-press on the phone's send, queues them for after instead: Edit or Take back until the turn ends, or Steer now.
4Esc stops now: the turn's open asks close and it ends "Stopped by you". Esc Esc rewinds this thread, ⇧Tab cycles four modes (Doesn't ask only by you), and / @ ! # open commands, files, shell and memory (the Composer board). Asks still sit inline at the tail and in Needs at once.
5kit's todos update live: in the side panel on the desktop, and as a "Todos 3 of 5" pill above the phone's composer that opens to the list. A long command moved to the background stays a pill with View output and Stop.
6Terminal is the same session's pty. One device owns the size; others watch until they tap Take size. The key bar shows only in Terminal, never in Session, where the phone composer keeps Stop and a steer send both in reach while kit streams.
7Smooth: steer is optimistic. The marker shows "steering" on send and confirms from thread.steered with steps and time. The transcript is an inverted list, the composer rides visualViewport, and 2,000 turns stay virtualized.
