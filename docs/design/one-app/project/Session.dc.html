How it works
1Vyre owns the session (ADR 0030). The chip names the provider the router picked, the model and the auth (Claude · opus · subscription, or Codex); a limit fallback to the API key shows as a line in the turn. The state word is one of starting, idle, running, waiting, stopped, failed. A session closed for idleness says "Resumes on your next message".
2Beautified, not hidden: every tool call is one quiet row with a verb and a mono summary. Runs fold into one line; a diff opens in place, additions on the lime wash, deletions on a neutral wash. Never red.
3Quiet work never looks stalled: a running row counts up (Running npm run build · 0:42), thinking collapses to its length, and each turn ends with its time and tokens.
4Asks and questions live inline at the tail and in Needs at once. Answer either one and the other resolves: the desktop shows the question answered from alex's iPhone.
5Words typed during a turn wait in Vyre's queue: Edit or Take back until the turn ends. Send now hands them to kit at its next step. Stop (Esc) interrupts: the turn's open asks close and it ends "Stopped by you". An ask from a Mac-owned session says "Answer it on alex's MacBook Pro" until federation forwards answers.
6Terminal is the same session's pty. One device owns the size; others watch at that size until they tap Take size. The key bar carries what a phone keyboard lacks.
