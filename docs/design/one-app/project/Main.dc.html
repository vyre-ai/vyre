Vyre one app · design sheet · 27 Sep 2026 · app-design

One app for the phone, the tablet and the desktop. The Capsule and the CLI speak the same words.

One Expo codebase for iOS, Android and the web, served by your own box. It covers what Paseo covers (coding agents with a real terminal) and the rest of Vyre: operating agents, their computers in Glass, the planner, the vault, devices, Tailscale and VyreDrive. It installs with no Apple Developer account.

1

Needs you comes first

Every surface opens on what waits for you, oldest first, one tap or one key from an answer. Violet marks it and nothing else.

2

One tree, three shapes

Phone, tablet and desktop are the same screens at different widths: swiped pages under 720, list and detail above, a side panel from 1400. No third layout.

3

Quiet chrome, loud content

Two fonts, five sizes, two weights, eight neutrals, one accent. Hierarchy comes from weight and tone. Tool calls are grey one-liners between readable prose.

4

The phone is a full client

Sessions, a real terminal with a key bar, Glass take-over, the vault and the planner all work on the phone. Nothing is desktop-only except window chrome.

5

No nagging

Face ID or Touch ID only to pair a device, release a vault secret, or send, post, pay or delete outside. One proof lasts 30 minutes and the screen says so.

6

Survive the network

Content stays on screen while one pill says "Reconnecting". Sends queue in an outbox and replay once. The app switches between Tailscale and the relay without asking.

7

Your box serves the app

The phone installs Vyre from your own address. No hosted page reaches into your machine, and no store or developer account stands between you and an update.

8

Written rules, shared parts

One tokens.json exports to Expo, the web and Swift. One row, one ask card, one status model, five buttons. A one-off needs a design review.
