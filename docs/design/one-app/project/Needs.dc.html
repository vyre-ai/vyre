How it works
1One row primitive for every kind: title (verb and object), one line of detail (the command in mono, the subject, the question), then kind · agent · project. The same row feeds the Capsule's list and vyre needs.
2Oldest first, so nothing starves. Violet is only the dot, the label and the badge. Cards stay neutral.
3Desktop: J and K move, the detail answers in place. Drafts edit in place, then Send (⌘⏎) or Discard (D). Asks take A, D and "Always in Harlow Legal".
4Phone: swipe right commits (the primary shows bone), left denies with a 4 s Undo. Tap opens the detail sheet with Open session.
5No nagging: one Face ID covers sends for 30 min, and the line under the buttons says so. Asks and questions never need a proof.
6Planner reminders join Needs when they fire and leave when done. Alarms ring as a banner on every device instead (Planner board).
7Smooth: the swipe is a scroll-snap row on the compositor, and the row collapses on the frame the swipe commits. The answer goes through the outbox with a 4 s Undo. The list paints from cache in under 1 s, before the box answers.
