Trust resets when a browser is paired again. A new pairing could be a new person with the same QR, so the browser starts limited until you say otherwise. Its old trust is not carried over.
How it works
1A browser paired from app.vyre.run is a device of kind web. Until you trust it, it can't pair devices, change trust, enroll or remove presence keys, or take a secret out (reveal, copy, render, resolve, release, export, fill, open a vault session). Everything else works, including the vault list.
2The card sits where Reveal and Copy would be, so the reason shows at the moment you need the secret. Names, kinds and sites stay readable.
3Ask to trust is a PROPOSED tool, not built yet (e.g. relay.devices.ask-trust): it puts one Device row in Needs you on your trusted devices. A second tap doesn't add a second row.
4Trust calls relay.devices.trust {id, trusted}. Trust asks Touch ID once; Stop trusting is one tap with no proof, because reducing trust protects you. Parked with relay: no proof on untrust, the name in the summary (today "Trust browser <id> fully").
5An untrusted browser can't call trust on itself, so you trust it from the Mac's Deck or Capsule, or from a trusted phone.
6Build known means the release and its manifest hash match a release this box ships with. Unknown keeps Trust, as an outline button, never primary. It catches mistakes, not a determined attacker.
7A browser expires after relay.web_expiry_days unused (default 30), counted from its last visit. Pairing again sets trust back to limited.
8Smooth: after Ask to trust, the button reads "Asked · waiting for your Mac" in place. When trust lands, the box closes the browser's channel ("trust changed"); the app reconnects quietly and the card turns into Reveal and Copy. No reload, no layout jump.
