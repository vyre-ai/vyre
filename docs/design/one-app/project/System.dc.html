System · one tokens.json, three exports

Plain values in one file become tokens.ts for the app, tokens.css for the Deck and Theme.swift for the Capsule.

The Deck's reduced system, unchanged in value: Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono, five sizes, two weights, eight neutrals, bone for action, violet for attention. From Paseo: a generated status model, three control heights and five buttons with one job each.

No red, coral, gold, honey or any other hue. A failure is a fact (text plus the crossed-circle glyph); a destructive action is slowed by words and a hold, never by colour. Teal replaces violet in one line per theme if chosen: #5FD4C4 dark, #0B6E66 paper.

one primary per surface

Primary the one action. Secondary a quiet fill for a common second choice. Outline a real alternative. Ghost Cancel, Deny, Discard, Details. Hold destructive: the label names the count, a 0.6 s hold fills it.Working keeps its width with a spinner and a verb. Disabled is label ink on a quiet fill, never opacity alone. Heights 28, 32, and 44 or 54 on touch.

Status, one model

most urgent wins
needs youViolet dot. Rows, tabs, favicon, app badge, the Capsule's mark, the CLI.
failedCrossed circle in text colour. Never pushes; waits in the list.
runningBone ring with an elapsed time, so quiet work never looks stalled.
unreadA text-colour dot. Clears on open, reply or send.
doneHollow dot. The row goes quiet.

Devices and hosts never get a colour: they are named ("alex's iPhone"), so a colour can only ever mean status.
