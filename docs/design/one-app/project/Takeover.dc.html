Rules
1No proof to take the keyboard. Take over, hand back and Sign in privately ask no passkey or Face ID. Agents, tailnet guests and Claude's own sessions are refused them outright, so no prompt ever appears.
2One keyboard. While someone holds it every other screen is read-only and Take over is disabled, its tooltip naming who and from where. The take-over follows the lease from laptop to phone.
3Idle hand-back: Off, 2, 5 or 15 min, 5 by default, in Settings, Network. Only typing, clicks and pointer moves reset it. The chip shows the last 10 s.
4The thread says who, how long, why it ended (gave back, idle, lapsed, released) and the note. Never what was typed.
5Screens, not viewers: 2 computers checked out at once by default. A third takes the least recently touched unwatched screen, or waits 30 s and names who holds each.
6Fill a login exists only in Sign in privately. In a plain take-over kit's link to Chrome stays open, so a filled password would be readable.
7Errors are neutral text and the failed glyph. Violet appears only as a needs-you dot, and none of these states needs you.
8Smooth: every state swaps in the strip above the screen, so the screen never jumps. The countdown ticks only while the page is visible; hand-back shows its notice on commit, and the screen canvas keeps its last frame through Starting.
