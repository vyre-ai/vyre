How it works
1Four choice cards. Balanced is preselected and tagged Recommended; Max is never preselected. Each card gives its two numbers, its peak and one plain line on who it is for.
2One unit is one Opus session working without pause; a helper counts 0.3. The impact line turns that into time: a 5-hour window lasts about 1 h 10 m on Balanced. It says estimate until the project has a week on the setting, then shows the measured average and peak.
3The meter is never red or orange, even at 9x. Heavy is said in words ("heavy, expect to hit your limit"); the fill stays lime for the chosen value.
4The box ceiling is read-only here. Max asks for 10 subagents, so on this box it runs as 8: the card says "Capped at 8 by the box" and the impact uses the capped numbers.
5The pause is on at 80 percent by default; the API-key fallback is off, because it turns a free wait into a bill. A pause shows once in Needs you and as the banner on Agents.
6Run now is person-only: it overrides the project limit once, never the box ceiling. Cancel collapses the row with a 4 s Undo.
7Smooth: every change applies optimistically through the outbox and "Saved" appears in place. The queue updates live from events, and rows keep their height when one moves, so nothing reflows.
