States · every list, every size

Every list has the same seven states, on the phone and on the desktop, and none of them leaves you without a next step.

Empty offers the first action. Loading keeps the row shapes. Offline keeps the content and queues what you send. Errors say what failed and offer a way out. Many, long and decided rows stay calm and neutral.
