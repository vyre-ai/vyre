How it works
1Two cadences. A sign-in per device (passkey, about 30 days, renews with use, 90 days at most) makes person actions work over HTTP: the web, the installed web app, app.vyre.run. Without one the box answers 401 person_session_required and the sheet opens.
2The sheet opens once, on the first person action, and names that action. After sign-in the action resumes; nothing is typed twice. No passkey on this device yet? "Add a passkey with a code from your Mac" enrolls one (vyre presence code), then the same sheet signs in with it. A phone paired over the relay signs in with its own device key (Face ID); the native app uses vyre://. End now is presence.session.close.
3A proof covers 30 min from presence.since. A lapse (403 presence_required) never opens a wall: Send becomes "Send with Face ID" and the line says when the last one was.
4Proofs only for pairing, vault secrets, and send, post, pay or delete outside. Answering, allowing an ask, take over and revoking never ask.
5Settings lists every signed-in device with its presence key. Revoke is immediate and needs no proof; expired rows stay until removed, so you see what lapsed.
6Smooth: the proof runs from the button already pressed, and the send commits through the outbox on the frame Face ID returns ("Sent", Undo 4 s). Coverage is read from the cached item, so the button label is right on first paint, with no full-screen interruption.
