How it works
1Mini frame: 16:10, radius 8, --code-bg, 1 px --rule-strong. The picture is letterboxed, never cropped. The small Live badge sits 8 in at the top left; no other chrome.
2Step line, 8 below: the status mark, the step's summary, a middle dot and the age. It truncates with an ellipsis; the full summary is the tooltip. A failed step adds why on a second line.
3Three variants: Card on Now (240, phone full width minus 32), Header in a thread (320, right aligned, collapsible with Hide screen and Show screen), Pill in the Capsule and the phone's collapsed thread.
4The data is sight (ADR 0036): sight.watch live on desktop, sight.frame stills on the phone, sight.stepped for the line.
5Step summaries are the acting module's own words, never screen text. The picture is aria-hidden; the line is a polite live region, at most once every 5 s ("kit: Clicked Compose in Mail").
6Smooth: the picture replaces in place with no fade; live frames never cross-fade. The step line swaps its text with a 150 opacity change and never slides. The ring stops under reduced motion.
