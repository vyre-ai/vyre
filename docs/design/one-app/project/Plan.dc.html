The mode chip says in plain words how much the agent may do without asking, and the floor and the Gate hold in every mode.

How it works
1Asks come in four kinds: permission, question, plan and mode (ADR 0030). ExitPlanMode is a plan: a neutral card at the tail and the row "kit has a plan to approve" in Needs. Answer either one and the other resolves.
2The plan renders as a short document: a title, numbered steps with paths in mono, what it will not touch, and the files it expects to change with neutral counts.
3Start building (⌘⏎) allows the plan and sets the mode to go on in: Asks first by default, or Edits allowed. Revise (R) opens the reply field prefilled "Change the plan: " and kit stays in Plan first. Keep planning declines with no note.
4Modes in plain words, the provider's name in mono. Claude: plan, default, acceptEdits. Codex maps its sandbox and approval policy to the same three (read-only, on-request, workspace-write) and a row with no match says so.
5Three modes only. Running with no asks is refused by design, so the popover and the sheet point to Edits allowed plus Always in the project. The floor and the Gate hold in every mode.
6Phone: steps fold after four, the composer tucks away while a plan waits, and the buttons stack with Start building at 54. ⇧Tab cycles modes on any keyboard.
7Smooth: Start building flips the card to "Building · Asks first" on the commit frame; the answer rides the outbox. A mode change is optimistic: the chip updates at once and reverts with a line if the box refuses.
