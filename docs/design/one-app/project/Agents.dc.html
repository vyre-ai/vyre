How it works
1Each agent has its own computer: a container on the box with a desktop, Chrome and a terminal (2 cores · 3 GB · 1440×900). None shows "Give juno a computer", which asks for no passkey. Restart and Limits (1 to 16 cores, 1 to 64 GB) sit on the panel.
2States: running, frozen, stopped, none. Frees its screen after about a minute with nobody watching, freezes 15 s later; opening wakes it. Frozen keeps the tabs.
3Glass has four jobs: Watch (default, read only), Take over (the one primary, T), Sign in privately, Files. No viewer cap: the cap is screens, 2 computers checked out at once.
4Take over pauses kit. Control comes back on Hand back (⌃⏎), after 5 min idle (Settings, Network, Glass hand-back; a chip warns 10 s before), or when the tab hides past its 90 s lease. The thread says who, how long, why.
5Sign in privately is a shielded take-over: kit can neither see nor act, so Fill a login lives only there. Take over, hand back and Sign in privately ask no passkey; agents are refused them outright.
6A slow link says relayed and drops to 5 fps rather than freezing. New agent is an outline so Take over stays the page's one primary.
7Smooth: Glass frames draw to a canvas, never as images in the DOM. Watching pauses when the tab or page hides, and a relayed link drops to 5 fps instead of stuttering.
