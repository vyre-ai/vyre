# Vyre 0.1.0

> [!GAP]
> This is a draft, written before the release is cut. Lines marked **(pending)** describe work
> that is built and tested on its own branch but not yet on main. They move into the release, or
> out of these notes, when 0.1.0 is tagged. `package.json` still says 0.0.1 until then.

## What Vyre is

Vyre is your partner for the work you do with Claude. It keeps your sessions, agents, memory and
vault running on a server you own, so the work carries on when you close the laptop, and it meets
you wherever you are: the Capsule on your Mac, the Deck in any browser, your phone. It remembers
what you told it, asks before anything leaves your hands, and stays out of the way the rest of the
time.

What you give it stays yours. The server holds it, Tailscale connects your devices, and only you
are let in. And every part is a module on one contract, so you can switch any of it off, swap
it, or build your own.

This is the first tagged release. Thank you for trying it.

## What is in it

### The Capsule, on your Mac

A command bar that opens when you press Control twice. Find files on the Mac and the server, ask your
assistant or any agent, answer what waits on you, fill a login from the vault, set timers and
send messages through the Mac's apps, and open Glass. It is a native app, built on your Mac by
`vyre capsule install`, with nothing to download.

- Asking about your screen: the Capsule attaches what is on screen only when your words point at
  it, and shows it as a chip first. Password fields and sign-in dialogs stay blind.
- Talking: `⌥⏎` and speak (needs a speech key in the vault).
- **(pending)** An answer appears on its own when you pause, from a fast model. `⌘⏎` thinks
  deeper, in the same thread.
- **(pending)** "do …": type `do` and what you want, and Vyre uses the Mac for you, step by step on
  screen. `Esc` stops it. Sends, payments and deletions still wait for you.

### The Deck, in any browser on your tailnet

Now (what needs you, what is running, what memory learned), projects and their sessions, Chat
with full Claude Code parity (steering, queueing, the `/` menu, `!` shell, `#` memory, images,
thinking, rewind), agents and their computers in Glass, the vault, memory as a floor plan, and a
terminal on the server that survives a lost connection.

### The phone

Open your server's address in Safari or Chrome and add it to the home screen: Now, Chat, Find, Glass
and approvals, with push notifications and quiet hours. **(pending)** `vyre phone add` pairs a
phone in one step, and the one app for web, iPhone and Android (ADR 0027) replaces the current
phone pages.

### Vyre IQ, your memory

Vyre reads your Claude Code sessions and keeps what matters as facts you can see, trace to the
turn they came from, pin, mute or correct. `vyre memory ask "…" --sources` answers questions about
your own work with the sources behind the answer. **(pending)** Vyre IQ (ADR 0034) answers only
from what you said yourself, gives the same answer each time, and says "I don't know yet" rather
than guess.

### The vault and connections

Secrets and logins, sealed on the server, sent only to the host they belong to, and revealed only
after Touch ID or a passkey. One proof covers 30 minutes. Google accounts connect with a browser
sign-in, and every email, post or payment waits at the Gate for you to send it. **(pending)**
Vault connections (ADR 0028): every key a module needs is set up from one "needs a credential"
flow, for API keys, OAuth, service accounts, mail servers and MCP servers, granted per surface.
**(pending)** "Send an email" offers every account you granted.

### Settings in one place

Deck Settings covers setup, the assistant, Claude Code, connections, the network, devices,
memory, notifications, security, modules and appearance. **(pending)** The settings hub (ADR
0035): every setting, session option and design token in one file, edited the same way from the
Deck, the phone and `vyre config`, at account or project level, and followed live by every
surface.

### Updates

`vyre box update` updates the server from the Mac. **(pending)** `vyre update` on the Mac reads
GitHub Releases, shows what changed, backs up your data first, and rolls back by itself if the
new version does not come up. `--channel beta` follows prereleases, and `--rollback` goes back
by hand.

### Modules and tips

Put a module in `~/.vyre/modules/` and it loads beside Vyre's own, with its tools reachable from
Claude, the CLI and every surface. The module API is versioned from this release (`apiVersion:
1`) and its types ship in `packages/module-sdk` **(pending)**. Every module ships tips: one short
line at a time about what you are using, what you have not tried, and what an update brought.
They never show while something waits for you, and they turn off in Settings. **(pending)**
`vyre tips` lists them now; each surface shows them as its next release lands.

## Known limits

- **Pairing a Mac needs a second device.** A Mac cannot approve its own pairing: approve it from
  your phone or another browser. See [known gaps](../known-gaps.md).
- **Federation is later.** The server reads your Mac's sessions and searches them, but answering a
  Mac session's question from the server, and typing into it from Chat, come in a later release.
- **The phone pages are being rebuilt.** The current pages work; the one app (ADR 0027) is on
  hold until the first real iPhone run.
- **No relay yet.** Phones and browsers reach the server over Tailscale. The relay that reaches it
  without Tailscale (ADR 0026) is built but not deployed.
- **Not yet checked on a real Mac:** computer use ("do …") and voice have passed their tests but
  not a first real run.
- **Not on npm yet.** Install from vyre.run (below). npm publishing comes after this release.
- **No new-module command yet.** `vyre module new`, `check` and `add` (ADR 0033 phases 1 and 3)
  come later; for now copy a module folder into `~/.vyre/modules/` and switch one off with
  `modules.disable` in config.json.

## Install

On your Mac:

```sh
npm install -g https://vyre.run/box/vyre.tgz
vyre up
```

`vyre up` asks where Vyre should run: a server you reach over SSH, this Mac, or a server you
already have. Onboarding then finishes in the browser: you, Claude, Tailscale, your address, your
history, your devices. Then build and open the Capsule:

```sh
vyre capsule
```

On a server of your own, Vyre installs with one line (`--dry-run` shows what it would change):

```sh
curl -fsSL https://vyre.run/install.sh | sh
```

The full walk-through is [Install](../get-started/install.md), and your first 20 minutes are
[Your first hour](../using/first-hour.md).

## Update

- The server, from your Mac: `vyre box update`.
- **(pending)** The Mac: `vyre update`, or `vyre update --check` to see whether one is waiting.
- After an update, `vyre tips new` shows what came with it.
