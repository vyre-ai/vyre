# Sidebar

The sidebar lists five places:

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Ask, what needs you, and tasks. See [Home](home.md). |
| Chats | One row per agent. Every chat with that agent is one of its threads. See [Chat](chat.md). |
| Projects | A project's chats, tasks, files, Flows and site. |
| Records | Contacts, projects, tasks and your own types, in Vyre's own store. |
| Files | The Space, projects and each agent's folder. See [Files](files.md). |

Vault and Settings sit at the foot. The top of the sidebar is Search, New chat, and fold.

You work in one Space at a time. The menu at the foot is the switcher. It shows the Space address and your role. Cmd 1 to 9 jumps to a Space. It also offers Add a Space, Space settings, Invite, You, Appearance, Lock and Sign out.

Settings, Sidebar can still reorder places, hide one, or pin a project or a saved view. Settings itself stays put, so you can undo a change. Your list is kept for you on the Space's server, so it is the same on every computer you use.
