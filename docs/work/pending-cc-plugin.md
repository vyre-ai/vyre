# Pending for docs/using/claude-code.md (cc-plugin), apply when cc-plugin merges to main

The lead's final /vyre text (27 Sep). It replaces the earlier parked text; there is no
"20 an hour" limit and no "only a person edits" line. Verify each line against the merged code.

- `/vyre todo <text>` adds a todo. `/vyre todo` with no text lists your open todos.
- `/vyre remind <when> <text>`, for example "6pm", "tomorrow 9am" or "in 20 minutes". Vyre's
  planner reads the time and says when the reminder will ring. With no time, Claude asks for one.
  The reminder arrives by push, the Capsule and the Deck, not in the Claude session.
- `/vyre agenda` shows today; `/vyre agenda tomorrow` shows another day.
- `/vyre remember <fact>` saves a fact to memory (memory.remember).
- `/vyre lesson <rule>` makes a lesson.
- Agents add notes, reminders and todos freely. An item shows who added it only when it did not
  come from you or your assistant.
